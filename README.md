​Overview & Clarification
​The Fleetmikewood Protocol is an open-access personal development framework exploring how structured prayer, self-hypnosis, and AI-assisted scripting can support stress reduction, mindfulness, and long-term well-being.
​Clarifying Earlier Posts: Epigenetic Regulation vs. Genetic Engineering
​Earlier public posts framed this framework under the broad banner of "redefining genetic engineering." To ensure clarity and scientific accuracy moving forward:
​Not Genetic Engineering or DNA Alteration: In biology, genetic engineering refers to direct molecular modification of an organism's DNA sequence. The Fleetmikewood Protocol does not alter, edit, or mutate DNA sequences.
​Focus on Epigenetic Gene Expression: The protocol focuses on epigenetics—the biological regulatory mechanisms (such as DNA methylation and histone modification) that act like dimmer switches to turn gene expression up or down in response to stress, environment, and daily habits.
​The Designer Prayers Framework (#DesignerPrayers)
​Designer Prayers and AI-assisted self-hypnosis scripts are structured cognitive and contemplative inputs designed to adapt to any personal spiritual worldview, concept of God, or secular mindfulness preference.

​"God, please upgrade my healing to my best self and extreme longevity. Thank you for my existence. Amen."
(Note: Scripts can be adapted with AI into secular self-hypnosis formats to match individual preference.)
​Scientific & Conceptual Context
​Established Mind-Body Research: Peer-reviewed research in psychoneuroimmunology and behavioral medicine shows that chronic stress, meditation, and contemplative practices can influence physiological stress responses and certain inflammatory pathways (such as NF-\kappaB) over time.
​Personal-Development Scope: While lifestyle and stress-reduction routines are well-studied for general health, using AI-assisted scripts or designer prayers to intentionally direct specific epigenetic marks or pass down chosen biological traits across generations is an experimental personal-development philosophy rather than an established clinical or biological mechanism.
